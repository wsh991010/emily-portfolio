Project Overview

Architecture
as a Story

Urban Hideaway explores how narrative techniques from films and literature can be translated into spatial experience. Through sequences of concealment, encounter, pursuit, and revelation, the project transforms a bookstore into a journey of self-discovery, where architecture becomes a medium for storytelling rather than simply a container for books.

  • Type Architecture
  • Design Tool AutoCAD
  • Modelling Rhino
01 / 06

The City of Masks

The city teaches people how to behave, what to say, and who to become. Over time, the distance between appearance and reality begins to disappear.

02 / 06

A Glimpse Beyond

Among familiar streets and everyday routines, a small opening appears. A place that seems ordinary, yet quietly invites another possibility.

03 / 06

Entering the Hideaway

The path narrows. The noise of the city fades behind layers of walls, trees, and shadows.

04 / 06

The Hidden World

Behind ordinary walls lies another reality. A place where time slows down, and forgotten parts of ourselves quietly return.

05 / 06

The Encounter

Not every encounter needs a destination. Sometimes a glance, a silhouette, or a shared moment of silence is enough to change a story.

06 / 06

Return

The path leads back to the city. But after crossing through hidden spaces and forgotten memories, the reflection in the mirror no longer feels the same.

Section

Plan

Designed as a late-night bookstore, the space combines quiet reading with anonymous social interaction, expressing themes of concealment and openness. The massing is shaped by site circulation and connected through elevated bridges overlooking the surrounding landscape.
