Phase 01

Site Analysis

Located in the dense urban fabric of Shilin, Taipei, this residential project explores how spatial enclosure can create privacy and environmental comfort within the city. The building mass is organised around a central courtyard to improve natural light, ventilation, and climatic responsiveness, while creating a protected interior landscape separated from the surrounding urban context.

Shared communal platforms are introduced at intermediate levels to encourage social interaction and strengthen the relationship between private and collective living within the vertical structure.

Plan

The ground floor is designed as a semi-public interface between the city and the residential community. Circulation routes, entrances, and landscaped areas guide movement into the central courtyard.

Phase 02

Section

The section illustrates how the enclosed building form frames a central courtyard, allowing light and views to penetrate deep into the block while supporting both privacy and shared living.

Design Process

VERTICAL
COMMUNITY

The project organises residential spaces around a central courtyard to create a quieter and more protected living environment within Taipei’s dense urban context. Layered communal platforms introduce natural light, ventilation, and opportunities for social interaction throughout the vertical structure.

Scroll

Spatial Strategies

The exterior responds to the dense urban surroundings, while the interior courtyard offers a contrasting atmosphere of calm and openness. This duality defines the spatial experience of the project.

Interaction

Shared spaces are distributed throughout the building to encourage informal encounters. Rather than prescribing interaction, the design allows varying degrees of visibility and proximity supporting both connection and privacy.

Material

A curated palette of concrete, stone, and timber defines the tactile quality of the project. Material contrasts are used to articulate spatial hierarchy while reinforcing a calm and cohesive architectural language.

Enclosure

The project is organised around an inward-facing courtyard, creating a sense of protection and intimacy. Solid volumes and framed openings define a spatial rhythm between enclosure and openness.

Landscape

The landscape is conceived as an extension of the architecture, introducing softness within a dense urban setting. Vegetation and terrain shape a central courtyard that mediates between built form and natural environment.
