Project Overview

The Warm
Living

Natural light enters from the side, casting soft shadows that highlight the curvature of furniture and the texture of fabrics and leather. A restrained colour palette is used to balance warm tones with neutral surfaces, creating depth without overwhelming the space.

  • Type Interior
  • Design Tool AutoCAD

Visual Strategy

VISUAL
STRATEGY

This study focuses on the interaction between materials and lighting in shaping interior atmosphere. Warm-toned materials, soft textiles, and reflective surfaces are combined to create visual richness and tactile depth. Directional lighting is used to cast defined shadows, emphasising form and surface texture, while softer ambient light maintains overall balance.
