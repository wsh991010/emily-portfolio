Project Overview

Environmental
Storytelling

This project explores how light can become a storytelling element within space. A single skylight introduces a focused beam that cuts through the darkness, revealing the miniature figure and transforming light into a physical presence. Through contrast, shadow, and atmosphere, the scene evokes a sense of stillness, solitude, and contemplation.

  • Type 3D Environment
  • Modelling Blender
  • composition AfterEffects

Process

Lighting
And Composition

The visual hierarchy is established through the relationship between illumination and shadow. Carefully controlled lighting directs the viewer’s gaze towards the central figure while revealing layers of depth throughout the environment, creating a balanced and cinematic composition.
